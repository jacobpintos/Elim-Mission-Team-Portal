Connecting your Facebook Page to the Team Portal

This lets our team app show your Page's public posts to mission team members inside the portal. It takes about ten minutes, and you only ever have to do it once.

⏱ ~10 minutes 💻 Use a computer if you can 🔁 One time only

What you're agreeing to

Read this part before anything else — it's the whole point of the request.

Our mission team has an app where volunteers see schedules, events and announcements. We'd like your Page's posts to appear there too, so team members see your updates without leaving the app.

To do that, Facebook needs you to give our app permission to read your Page — we can't do it from our side. The permission you'll grant is read-only, and it covers only things already visible to the public on your Page.

What this allows

  • ✓Reading posts you've published publicly
  • ✓Reading the photos attached to those posts
  • ✓Seeing how many likes and comments a post has
  • ✓Reading your Page's name and profile picture

What this does not allow

  • ✕Posting, editing or deleting anything as your Page
  • ✕Reading your Page's messages or inbox
  • ✕Touching ads, billing or payment information
  • ✕Seeing your personal profile, friends or private activity
You stay in control

You can disconnect at any moment, without asking us — instructions are at the bottom of this page. Nothing about your Page changes when you connect, and your followers see no difference.

Before you start

Check all four. If any one is missing, contact us before continuing.

  • You can log in to the Facebook account that manages the Page Not the Page itself — the personal account you use to administer it.
  • You have full control of the Page If you're only an Editor or Moderator, the Page won't appear in step 7 and someone with full control will need to do this instead.
  • You're on a laptop or desktop computer It works on a phone, but the screens are cramped and easy to mis-tap. A computer is much easier.
  • You have the link we sent you A web address ending in our portal's name. If you don't have it, ask us for it before starting.

Part one — Accept our invitation

A one-time step that tells Facebook we're allowed to ask. Do this first, or part two will fail.

  1. Log in to Facebook as the account that manages the Page

    This matters more than it sounds. Many people have more than one Facebook login, or are signed in as a different Page. If you're not sure, click your profile picture in the top corner and check the name shown.

  2. Go to the invitations page

    In the same browser, visit developers.facebook.com/requests. You'll see a list of pending invitations — ours will be from an app called [APP NAME].

    If the page looks empty

    An empty list almost always means you're logged in as a different account than the one we invited. Switch accounts and reload. If it's still empty, tell us and we'll re-send the invitation.

  3. Click Confirm on our invitation

    You're accepting a role called Tester. That's just Facebook's word for "this person is allowed to connect their Page to this app." It gives us nothing on its own — the actual permission comes in part two.

  4. If Facebook asks you to agree to developer terms, accept

    Facebook may ask you to register as a developer and agree to their policies, and it may ask you to confirm your email or phone number. This is a formality Facebook requires for anyone accepting an app invitation. It doesn't change your account, doesn't cost anything, and doesn't make anything public.

Part two — Connect the Page

The actual connection. Stay logged in as the same account.

  1. Open the link we sent you

    Paste it into the same browser where you just accepted the invitation: [CONNECT LINK]

  2. Read the page, then click Continue to Facebook

    The page explains what's being shared before anything happens. When you click through, everything until step 10 happens on Facebook's own website, not ours.

    Never share your password

    If Facebook asks you to log in, you're typing your password into facebook.com — never into our app, and never into an email or text message. Nobody from our team will ever ask you for your Facebook password, or for a code, token or screenshot of one. If anyone does, it isn't us.

  3. Choose your Page — and make sure it's ticked

    Facebook shows a list of Pages you manage with checkboxes. Tick the box next to the Page you want connected, then continue. If your Page isn't in the list, you don't have full control of it — stop here and tell us.

    The most common mistake

    People click straight through this screen without ticking anything. Facebook accepts that quite happily and then connects nothing at all. Look for the tick before moving on.

  4. Leave every switch turned on

    The next screen lists what our app is asking for, each with a toggle. They're all on by default — leave them that way. Turning any of them off breaks the connection, and the error Facebook shows afterwards won't explain why.

    These are the read-only permissions described at the top of this page. Nothing here lets us post as your Page.

  5. Click Done, then OK

    Facebook usually shows a confirmation screen after you finish. Click through it and the window will close by itself.

  6. Look for the green tick

    You'll land back on our page, and it should say your Page name is connected. That's the confirmation — your posts will start appearing in the team app within a few minutes. You can close the window.

    If you manage several Pages

    You'll get one extra screen first, asking which Page to connect. Pick the one you want in the team app — the others are left completely untouched.

  7. Let us know you're done

    Send a quick message to [YOUR NAME AND CONTACT] so we can confirm it's working on our end. That's everything — you won't need to do this again.

If something goes wrong

The four problems that actually come up.

The invitations page is empty

You're signed in as a different Facebook account than the one we invited. Click your profile picture, check which account you're using, switch if needed, and reload.

My Page isn't in the list

Facebook only offers Pages you have full control of. If you're an Editor or Moderator, whoever holds full control will need to do this instead — or give you full control first.

It says "Something went wrong"

Nearly always a permission switch that got turned off in step 8. Start again from step 5 and leave all of them on. If it happens twice, send us a screenshot of the error.

It said Connected, but no posts are showing

Give it fifteen minutes — the first sync isn't instant. After that, tell us, and we'll look from our side. Don't redo these steps; connecting twice doesn't help.

Disconnecting later

You don't need our permission, and you don't need to tell us first.

On Facebook, go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations. Find [APP NAME] in the list and choose Remove. Access stops immediately.

Posts already showing in the team app will stop updating, and nothing on your Facebook Page is affected in any way. A note to us afterwards would be appreciated so we're not troubleshooting a problem that doesn't exist.

Questions before you start are very welcome — it's easier to answer them now than to untangle a half-finished connection later. Reach us at [YOUR NAME AND CONTACT].
